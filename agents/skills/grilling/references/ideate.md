# Ideate

For a raw idea that is not yet a plan. Diverge first, then run the standard interview on the chosen variant.

1. Restate the idea as a "How might we" problem.
2. Ask the sharpening questions as the first round: who it is for, what success looks like, the real constraints, what was tried before, why now.
3. Generate 5-8 variants. Name the lens on each:
   - **Inversion**: do the opposite.
   - **Constraint removal**: drop budget, time, or technology limits.
   - **Audience shift**: serve a different user.
   - **Combination**: merge with an adjacent idea.
   - **Simplification**: the version 10x simpler.
   - **10x version**: the idea at massive scale.
   - **Pre-mortem**: assume it failed; what is the variant that avoids that?
   - **Analogous inspiration**: how another domain solves the same problem.
   - **Constraint-based**: force a hard limit, such as one screen or one day.
4. The user picks one or merges several. Do not choose for them.
5. Run the standard interview on the pick.

The brief adds two sections: **Variants considered** and **Not doing**, each with its reason.
