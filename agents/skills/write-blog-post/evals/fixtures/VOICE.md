## Contextual examples

These examples are a notebook, not a required outline or sentence formula.
Use the structure the reader needs. Jokes, asides, sentence lengths, and
exclamations have no quotas.

"Why keep the cache?" can introduce an explanation of a personal choice.
"I may keep it" preserves uncertainty; it does not mean "I will keep it".
Connect technical claims to available evidence. Keep an unavailable result
open rather than inventing a measurement or a closing lesson.
